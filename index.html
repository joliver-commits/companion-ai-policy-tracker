The distinction that matters most in this corpus. Asking “does this bill target companion apps or general chatbots?” conflates three separate questions: the term the instrument uses, the test that determines what the term covers, and the narrowing device that pulls things back out again. They do not line up.

An open research dataset covering proposed, active and enacted policy; instruments that die, are vetoed or go inactive are removed.
