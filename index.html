Which regulatory mechanisms each piece of legislation actually carries. The counts below are how much legislation touches each mechanism at all — they say nothing about how well. Read them alongside the Gaps view, where the thin rows are the argument.

The sixteen mechanisms group into five clusters. Comparing the clusters is usually the more informative read: honesty and harm-response obligations are near-universal, while the cluster that constrains the product's own design is nearly empty.

The distinction that matters most in this corpus. Asking “does this bill target companion apps or general chatbots?” conflates three separate questions: the term the legislation uses, the test that determines what the term covers, and the narrowing device that pulls things back out again. They do not line up.

An open research dataset covering proposed, active and enacted policy; legislation that dies, is vetoed or goes inactive is removed.
